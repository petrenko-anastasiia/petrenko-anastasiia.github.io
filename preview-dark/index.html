[ How I work ]

Write the hypothesis, test the hooks, cut the losers, scale the winners.

I run creative testing on Meta and TikTok for DTC lifestyle subscriptions & creator-led brands, with budget, creative and testing owned end to end.

  1. Every test starts with a written hypothesis.
  2. I build creative variants per hook and format and test them in Meta Ads Manager.
  3. I cut the losing hooks and move the budget to the winners.
  4. Around 8–10 new creatives a week: hooks, angles and UGC-style variations.

Meta campaign · DTC lifestyle subscription brand$48 $22 per paid subscription checkout over 6 weeks (~54% lower). I moved the ad hooks from lifestyle to product demo, tested the variants and scaled the hook type that converted.

I research what works in the market, define how the brand should sound, turn it into a publishing plan, then check the numbers and adjust.

  1. Market and competitor research: the formats and angles that drive reach in a niche.
  2. Positioning and tone of voice for each account.
  3. Calendars, briefs and workflows a small team can run every week.
  4. I drop what underperforms and do more of what works.

Results1M+ organic views in one month across up to 3 accounts. Client accounts are private, so I show results, not handles.

Concept, brief, then TikTok or Reels video. I use AI video tools for visuals and Claude for copy variants.

The spec ads above are made for fictional brands and labelled as AI. Postroom, the publishing tool I built in Cursor, brings AI caption generation, approval, scheduling and Instagram API publishing into one workspace.
