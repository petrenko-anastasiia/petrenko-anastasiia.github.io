01 · Paid social

Paid social

For four years I ran content and paid social for DTC lifestyle subscriptions & creator-led brands. That meant creative testing on Meta and TikTok, short-form production for TikTok and Reels, and a content team behind it.

  • ~$43Kpaid spend in October 2026 → 34K sign-ups, including free and trial (~$1.25 each)

  • 8–10new creatives tested a week, cut or scaled by hook type
6-week A/B test→

Frames are stand-ins from my Hook Lab pieces — the campaign creatives are private. CPA = cost per acquisition (paid subscription checkout on Meta).

Meta campaign · DTC lifestyle subscription brand
~54% lower CPA in 6 weeks

Setup. Meta Ads Manager, subscription brand, same audiences and budgets on both sides; ad sets split by hook type, 8–10 new variants a week. Every variant started from a written hypothesis.
Decision. Losers were cut on CPA per paid checkout; budget moved to the product-demo hook type, then scaled.
Result. CPA ~$48 → ~$22 over six weeks (~54%), ROAS (return on ad spend) held at 2.5–4.0×.
