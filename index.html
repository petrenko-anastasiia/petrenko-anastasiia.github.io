02 — Content systems

Consistency is a system, not willpower.

I set up the calendar, the briefs and the hand-offs so a small team publishes every week without last-minute scrambles — and so every post feeds the next decision.

  1. Research

    Trends, competitors and what's already performing in the niche.

  2. Brief

    Hook, format, tone and CTA written down before anyone films or generates.

  3. Produce

    Filmed or AI-generated, batched for the week, checked against the brand look.

  4. Review

    Weekly numbers: kill the weak formats, scale the winners.
