Spec ad · AI / Golden Body Oil / Creative brief

Summer in one bottle.

Fictional brand/8 s 9:16 video + 4:5 key visual/Concept work, not a real client

Brand & product

ARGŌ Golden Body Oil is a fictional brand created for this spec ad. The product is a shimmering dry body oil that gives an instant sun-kissed glow on legs and shoulders. It comes in a compact, rounded glass bottle filled with golden mica, closed with a fluted gold cap.

Objective

Make a scroller understand within three seconds what the product does and why they would want it: instant golden glow. Build awareness and desire first; the next step is to open the product page.

Target audience

Women 22–35 who buy beauty products on social. They want a glow for holidays, beach days and going-out evenings, and they discover products on TikTok, Instagram Reels and Pinterest.

Key insight

Everyone has seen a shimmer-oil ad in which someone simply rubs oil on a leg, and nobody remembers which one. Glow is hard to describe in words, but it is very easy to recognise when you see it catch the light.

Single-minded proposition

“Summer in one bottle.”

Concept: “Your leg is the beach”

Tiny, real-looking adults treat the oil like a luxury golden holiday. In the finished ad, two women float on white loungers in a pool of gold shimmer oil around the bottle, a sunbather lies on a pebble island and a lifeguard in red watches from his chair. The key visual plays the same idea on a woman's shin: her leg is the beach. The benefit, glow, is shown, not told. If one drop can fill a pool, the bottle holds the whole summer.

Tone & visual direction

  • Warm late-afternoon sun, cream linen, a gold palette.
  • Natural white balance: rich gold and skin tones, no orange cast.
  • Macro lens, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, editorial luxury.
  • The tiny people must read as real humans, not toys: real skin, hair and fabric, natural poses.
  • A compact, rounded bottle with a fluted gold cap: an original design, not a copy of any existing brand.

Mandatories

  • One bottle only, in every frame.
  • Label legible: “ARGŌ” and “GOLDEN BODY OIL”.
  • “Spec ad · AI” visible on every deliverable.
  • No claims beyond “glow”: the oil does not promise a tan or any skin result.
  • No on-screen text inside the generated clips; the end line and tag are added in the edit.

What I would test

Three hook variants with the same product, captions and end card:

  • A · Drop open: the finished cut, starting on the extreme close-up of the oil drop and its sound.
  • B · Pool open: skip the macro and open straight on the golden pool and the tiny people.
  • C · Alternative line: the same cut with “Vacation in a bottle.” as the end line.

Measure the 3-second hold (thumb-stop), overall hold rate and click-through, and compare the variants against each other. This is a plan: no campaign has run, and no results are claimed.

AI workflow

  • Brief and prompts: drafted with Claude, then edited by hand.
  • Stills: the key visual and the pool start frame were prompted in Gemini (Nano Banana), iterating on the realism of the miniatures and the shape of the bottle.
  • Video: Veo image-to-video from the pool still for the wide shot, and a separate Veo clip for the macro drop.
  • Edit: the two clips joined on the splash, then the end line and “Spec ad · AI” tag added in the edit (ffmpeg, CapCut-style).

The finished ad is the 8-second video at the top of this page. The label goes a little soft in the wide shot, which comes from the generated clip.
