Spec ad · AI / Golden Body Oil / Creative brief

Summer, bottled.

Fictional brand/4:5 key visual + 9:16 Reel/Concept work, not a real client

Brand & product

ARGŌ Golden Body Oil is a fictional brand created for this spec ad. The product is a shimmering dry body oil that gives an instant sun-kissed glow on legs and shoulders. It comes in a compact, rounded glass bottle filled with golden mica, closed with a fluted gold cap.

Objective

Make a scroller understand within three seconds what the product does and why they would want it: instant golden glow. Build awareness and desire first; the next step is to open the product page.

Target audience

Women 22–35 who buy beauty products on social. They want a glow for holidays, beach days and going-out evenings, and they discover products on TikTok, Instagram Reels and Pinterest.

Key insight

Everyone has seen a shimmer-oil ad in which someone simply rubs oil on a leg, and nobody remembers which one. Glow is hard to describe in words, but it is very easy to recognise when you see it catch the light.

Single-minded proposition

“Summer, bottled.”

Concept: “Your leg is the beach”

Tiny, real-looking people treat a woman's glowing skin like a luxury golden beach: one sunbathes on a towel, one rolls the oil into the skin like he is polishing it, one dips her feet into a droplet of oil beside the bottle. The benefit, glow, is shown, not told. If the skin can be a holiday, the oil is the holiday.

The 8-second hook, in three beats.

  1. 0–2 s · The dropStill, warm moment. Golden mica swirls inside the bottle and catches the sun. The tiny girl at the base of the bottle kicks her feet in the oil droplet and makes a small glittering splash.Job: stop the thumb with texture and sound.
  2. 2–5 s · The glowThe tiny man rolls his roller along the shin and leaves a glowing streak of shimmer that spreads and sparkles. The sunbather lifts her sunglasses, looks up at the camera and smiles.Job: show the benefit and add a human moment.
  3. 5–8 s · The revealThe camera slowly pulls back and up to show the whole glowing leg in late-afternoon sun, with the ARGŌ bottle sharp and centred and the label readable.Job: product recall and the end card.

Tone & visual direction

  • Warm late-afternoon sun, cream linen, a gold palette.
  • Natural white balance: rich gold and skin tones, no orange cast.
  • Macro lens, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, editorial luxury.
  • The tiny people must read as real humans, not toys: real skin, hair and fabric, natural poses.
  • An elongated, fluted-gold bottle: an original design, tall and slim, not a copy of any existing brand.

Mandatories

  • One bottle only, in every frame.
  • Label legible: “ARGŌ” and “GOLDEN BODY OIL”.
  • “Spec ad · AI” visible on every deliverable.
  • No claims beyond “glow”: the oil does not promise a tan or any skin result.
  • No on-screen text inside the generated video; captions are added in the edit.

What I would test

Three hook variants with the same product, captions and end card:

  • A · Drop ASMR open: the extreme close-up of the oil drop and the sound.
  • B · Sunglasses look: open on the tiny sunbather lifting her sunglasses to the camera.
  • C · Pull-back reveal: open on the product and the glowing leg, then reveal the tiny people.

Measure the 3-second hold (thumb-stop), overall hold rate and click-through, and compare the variants against each other. This is a plan: no campaign has run, and no results are claimed.

AI workflow

  • Brief and prompts: drafted with Claude, then edited by hand.
  • Key visual: prompted in Gemini (Nano Banana), then iterated on the realism of the miniatures and the shape of the bottle.
  • Video: Veo image-to-video from the key visual, so the bottle and people stay consistent.
  • Edit: CapCut for captions and the end card.
