Real brand · Brow Soap Mini · 25 g

Brows with attitude.

A Ukrainian-language product ad about shape, direction, and personal expression.

15-second ad9:16 verticalUkrainian copy

The brief

Make ZOLA Brow Soap Mini recognizable to Ukrainian-speaking beauty shoppers and invite them to explore the product on the official store.

The hook · First 3 seconds

«Брови з характером?»

“Brows with attitude?”

The idea

«Задай їм напрям.» — Give them direction.
Upward motion makes direction the visual motif. Pink, white, and black echo the official packaging. The story moves from personality to product to one clear action.

The product

ZOLA Brow Soap Mini, 25 g: a soap for styling brow hairs. The ad uses the official product photo and avoids fabricated before-and-after results.

The next step

«Відкрий Brow Soap» — Discover Brow Soap.
Visit the official product page ↗

What I would test

Test the personality-led hook against a mini-format product hook. Compare early retention and product-page click-through, keeping the rest of the ad fixed.
