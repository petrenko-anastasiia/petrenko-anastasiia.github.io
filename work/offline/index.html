Spec campaign · Focus app

Less scroll.
More you.

A familiar moment of distraction becomes a simple invitation to focus.

15-second social ad9:16 verticalMotion design

The brief

Introduce a fictional focus app to students and early-career creatives who open their phones for one thing and get pulled into everything else. Encourage them to start one 25-minute session.

The hook · First 3 seconds

“You opened your phone
for one thing.”

The creative decision

Notifications crowd the frame, then give way to a single timer. The change from visual noise to space makes the benefit visible before the final call to action. The story works without sound.

The next step

Start your first 25 minutes.
One specific, approachable action.

What I would test

Compare this opening with “Where did the last 25 minutes go?” Measure early retention and first-session completion. Keep the rest of the ad the same.

Fictional brand. Self-initiated spec work; concept interface. No affiliation or performance results claimed. No character-consistency claim.
